# FINAL THAW — Character Bible

## Dr. Elena Vast

### Role
Atmospheric systems scientist; co-developer of the Aster Protocol; puzzle protagonist.

### External goal
Reach Final Thaw Station and activate Aster before the atmospheric feedback loop becomes unrecoverable.

### Internal need
Accept that correct technical action alone is not sufficient; survival infrastructure must be accountable to the people who depend on it.

### Wound / pressure
Elena spent years producing warnings inside institutions that absorbed, delayed, and buried them. She believes decisiveness is a moral virtue because delay has already cost lives.

### Strengths
- Observant, methodical, technically inventive.
- Reads infrastructure as a system of causes and consequences.
- Will take personal risk when the alternative is abandoning people.

### Flaws
- Can become impatient with emotional or political arguments when time is scarce.
- Uses precision as distance from grief.
- Initially mistakes Marcus's directness for simple-mindedness.

### Arc
Elena begins convinced that delivering Aster is the sole ethical objective. She ends understanding that deployment, access, evidence, and governance are inseparable.

### Voice
Precise but not robotic. Uses concrete observations under stress. Avoids speeches. Her rare emotional admissions should be short and specific.

### Sample voice
- “That pump is not failing. It is being starved of power.”
- “I do not need you to agree with me. I need that door open.”
- “A solution nobody can reach is not a solution.”

### Gameplay expression
Scan, interact, reroute, calibrate, reveal. Her actions make previously impossible routes possible.

## Marcus Reyes

### Role
Former police officer; combat/protection protagonist.

### External goal
Keep Elena and the prototype alive long enough to reach the relay, then prevent Helix from controlling the outcome.

### Internal need
Move beyond guilt and reactive protection into accountable action. He cannot erase his role in emergency enforcement, but he can choose what he does with his knowledge now.

### Wound / pressure
Marcus remained in the force during emergency rationing because he believed someone inside could limit harm. Over time, he enforced systems that displaced and injured people. Helix's capture order forces him to confront how far the institution has already moved.

### Strengths
- Reads immediate physical danger and crowd dynamics.
- Practical, decisive, protective without being sentimental.
- Understands Helix tactics, command language, and security infrastructure.

### Flaws
- Defaults to action before explanation.
- Carries shame as anger and sarcasm.
- Initially judges Elena as another expert who saw the crisis coming but failed to stop it.

### Arc
Marcus begins as an extractor following an order. He becomes a collaborator who recognises protection means more than keeping one person alive; it means protecting access, evidence, and people outside the mission.

### Voice
Short, direct sentences. Dry humor appears only under pressure. Avoid melodramatic confession.

### Sample voice
- “They did not send me to help. They sent me to contain.”
- “You fix the machine. I will make sure it stays fixed.”
- “If Helix owns the cure, they own the queue.”

### Gameplay expression
Clear threat, hold space, create safe windows, move obstacles, defend Elena during exposed work.

## Helix Retrieval Commander

### Role
Final antagonist and embodiment of Helix operational logic.

### Goal
Secure Aster and Final Thaw Station for controlled deployment.

### Belief
Unmanaged survival causes chaos; hierarchy is regrettable but necessary. This belief is coherent enough to be dangerous, not cartoonish.

### Voice
Calm, procedural, certain. Does not shout until defeat is close.

### Sample voice
- “A system that serves everyone serves no one for long.”
- “You call it control because you have never had to allocate oxygen.”

### Design rule
The Commander is not the source of all evil. Defeating them does not automatically dismantle Helix.

## Supporting Groups

### Shelter residents
Not a single faceless population. Include practical organizers, frightened families, exhausted maintenance workers, and people who disagree about whether to trust Elena or Marcus.

### Port evacuees
People confronting selective evacuation. Their optional rescue scenes should show agency: they help others, share routes, or protect evidence rather than merely wait to be saved.

### Earth Systems Restoration Network
Dispersed researchers and technicians. Most are absent, dead, missing, or communicating through fragments. Their traces make Aster feel collaborative rather than the work of one genius.
