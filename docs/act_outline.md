# FINAL THAW — Act and Phase Outline

## Act I: Separation

### Phase 0 — Technical Foundation / Playable framing
A monitoring facility floods as alarms fail. This establishes that climate collapse is an active physical condition, not background lore.

### Phase 1 — Elena: Movement and Observation
Elena leaves with Aster. Her immediate objective is survival and access, not combat. The player learns that she reads infrastructure as evidence.

### Phase 2 — Abandoned Laboratory
Elena restores power, moves a robotic arm, uses a platform, and carries Aster through calibration hazards. She obtains proof that Helix delayed viable deployment.

### Phase 3 — Marcus: Combat Fundamentals
Marcus receives an extraction order. A rationing queue becomes violence after Helix contractors restrict water access. Marcus protects civilians, signaling moral conflict before he verbalizes it.

### Phase 4 — Highway Riots
Marcus clears a route through contested supply infrastructure. He finds that his unit has been reassigned to capture Elena.

### Phase 5 — Broken Vehicle
Repair minigame. The fire front makes the emergency real without punishing the player with a hard fail.

### Phase 6 — Flooded Shelter
Elena can rescue people trapped by flooding. Civilian Aid establishes that her path includes choices beyond the prototype.

### Phase 7 — Militia Encirclement
Marcus crosses Helix blockade and defeats a former commander. He discovers the new order: terminate Elena if capture fails.

### Phase 8 — First Contact
Marcus finds Elena. She reads him as Helix. He tells her the truth before he can prove it. They escape into toxic rain.

**Act turn:** They now share a route, not trust.

## Act II: Cooperation

### Phase 9 — Calibrate Aster
Inside the vehicle, Elena identifies Final Thaw Station. Helix's alternative plan is revealed: stabilise protected zones, abandon the rest.

### Phase 10 — Collapsing Dam
Elena stabilises a route through failing infrastructure. Her expertise is shown as immediate survival work.

### Phase 11 — Port Mutiny
Marcus sees evacuation intentionally restricted. Optional aid scenes demonstrate that people are more than extraction objectives.

### Phase 12 — Free Switching
The transit hub requires both protagonists. Their tactical cooperation becomes fluent. Their political disagreement becomes explicit: immediate deployment versus accountable deployment.

**Act turn:** They discover that Helix's control is not merely a future risk; it was built through deliberate sabotage.

## Act III: The Final Thaw

### Phase 13 — Final Thaw Station
The pair enters the occupied mountain relay. Snow becomes blizzard, then extreme storm. They can aid researchers, restore systems, and uncover evidence of Helix sabotage. The final evidence choice is made.

### Phase 14 — Helix Retrieval Commander
The fight turns the relationship into mechanics: Elena calibrates; Marcus protects openings. Victory creates time for activation, not instant political resolution.

### Phase 15 — Choice of Spring
Aster activates. Ending reflects integrity, aid, safety, and evidence.

### Phase 16 — QA / Release
Production phase only; no new canonical story.
