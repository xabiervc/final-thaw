# FINAL THAW — Sample Dialogue

This document provides example dialogue for key story beats. Use these as tone references, not scripts to copy verbatim.

## General Dialogue Rules

- Keep lines short and purposeful. Avoid monologues unless the situation demands them.
- Let subtext carry part of the meaning. Characters do not always say exactly what they feel.
- Climate collapse is the condition, not the topic of every sentence.
- Dialogue should be skippable and must not block gameplay.

---

## Act I: Separation

### Phase 1 — Elena Escapes Monitoring Facility

**Context:** Alarms fail. Water rises. Elena grabs the Aster prototype.

**Elena (internal, optional):**
- "If the warnings worked, I would not be here."
- "Move first. Think later."

**Elena (to herself, when power flickers):**
- "Of course. The one circuit that matters."

---

### Phase 4 — Marcus on Highway, Discovers Reassignment

**Context:** After clearing an arena, Marcus finds a Helix datapad.

**Marcus (reading, quiet):**
- "Extract and secure. Standard."
- (scrolls) "Terminate if capture fails."
- "They do not even pretend anymore."

**Marcus (to himself, before next arena):**
- "One more job. Then I decide what kind of job."

---

### Phase 8 — First Contact in Flooded Shelter

**Context:** Marcus breaches the shelter. Elena is behind a protected terminal.

**Marcus:**
- "Target located. Approaching."

**Elena:**
- "You are Helix. Stay back."

**Marcus:**
- "Not anymore. They changed orders. We need to move."

**Elena (after first gate opens):**
- "You expect me to trust you because Helix lied to you?"

**Marcus:**
- "I expect you to trust that I do not want to die in a flooded shelter."

**Elena (small pause):**
- "That, at least, is clear."

**Exit sequence (toxic rain):**

**Marcus:**
- "Temporary truce. We escape together, then decide next steps."

**Elena:**
- "Agreed. But I choose when and where the protocol goes."

**Marcus:**
- "As long as it goes somewhere Helix cannot reach immediately, we are fine."

## Act II: Cooperation

### Phase 9 — Calibrate Aster in Damaged Vehicle

**Context:** Elena works on the circuit board. Marcus drives through the storm.

**Elena (focused):**
- "Path incomplete. Rerouting."
- "There. That should hold."

**Marcus (from driver seat, dry):**
- "Try not to blow us up before I get us out of this storm."

**Elena (without looking up):**
- "If I wanted us dead, I would have stayed with Helix."

**On completion:**

**Elena:**
- "Prototype recalibrated. Destination confirmed: Final Thaw Station."

**Marcus:**
- "And Helix?"

**Elena:**
- "Preparing a competing intervention. Protected zones only."

**Marcus:**
- "So the world stabilises for the people they already decided matter."

**Elena:**
- "Precisely. Which is why we do not let them decide."

---

### Phase 12 — Transit Hub, Free Switching, Core Disagreement

**Context:** After solving the final room together.

**Elena:**
- "Your methods are crude, but effective."

**Marcus:**
- "Your plans need someone to watch your back."

**Marcus (after evidence appears):**
- "They engineered the test failure. They built the crisis they are now solving."

**Elena:**
- "Then deployment becomes political whether we like it or not."

**Marcus:**
- "If we deploy through Helix, we stabilise the climate and hand them the queue."

**Elena:**
- "If we delay to expose them, people die while we argue."

**Marcus:**
- "Then we do both. But someone has to choose which comes first."

**Choice prompt (preserved in GameManager):**
- Preserve evidence (delay briefly, expose Helix)
- Erase evidence (prioritise immediate activation)

## Act III: The Final Thaw

### Phase 13 — Final Thaw Station, Evidence Discovery

**Context:** In a secured archive room.

**Elena (reading):**
- "Test 7-C. Deliberately misreported. They knew it would work."

**Marcus:**
- "So they manufactured the failure to justify emergency authority."

**Elena:**
- "And to ensure that when Aster finally deployed, they owned it."

**Marcus:**
- "Do we expose them and risk delay, or deploy now and let them own the story?"

**Elena (quiet):**
- "I spent years believing the data would speak for itself. It does not. People decide what to hear."

**Marcus:**
- "Then we make sure the right people hear the right version. Even if it costs us time."

*(Player makes final evidence choice here.)*

### Phase 14 — Final Boss Confrontation

**Context:** Helix Retrieval Commander enters atmospheric control chamber.

**Commander:**
- "You think you can override decades of planning with a prototype and a conscience?"

**Marcus:**
- "No. Just your monopoly on who gets to live."

**Commander (during fight):**
- "A system that serves everyone serves no one for long."

**Elena (mid-calibration):**
- "A system that serves only some is not a recovery. It is a filter."

**Commander (low health):**
- "You do not understand what you are destroying."

**Marcus:**
- "I understand exactly what I am stopping."

**On defeat:**

**Commander:**
- "Perhaps you are right. But the world will not thank you for this."

**Marcus:**
- "It does not need to. It needs to exist."

## Endings

### Public Thaw

**Narration:**
- "The Aster Protocol activates. Storms weaken. Atmospheric feedback slows."
- "Communities receive the stabilisation data. Helix loses public legitimacy."
- "Elena and Marcus release the protocol openly. Recovery begins, not as a commodity, but as a shared resource."
- "The world does not heal instantly. But for the first time in years, spring feels possible."

---

### Guarded Thaw

**Narration:**
- "The Aster Protocol activates. Climate collapse slows."
- "But Helix controls access to recovery technology. Protected zones thrive; others remain vulnerable."
- "Elena's data is proprietary. Marcus's defection is classified."
- "The world stabilises, but the question remains: stability for whom?"

---

### Fragile Thaw

**Narration:**
- "The Aster Protocol activates imperfectly. Storms weaken but do not cease."
- "Elena is injured. The prototype is damaged. Recovery is possible, but harder."
- "Helix retains control. Communities must rebuild with limited resources."
- "Survival remains possible. But the cost was high, and the compromises ahead are harder."

## Optional Rescue Dialogue Examples

- "We thought the pumps were dead."

**Elena:**
- "They were. They are not anymore."

**Resident:**
- "There are others below. If the water rises again…"

**Elena:**
- "Then I will make sure it does not. Not today."
